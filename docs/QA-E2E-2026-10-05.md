# QA Evidence — end-to-end trace of one NGO and one donor

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Branch:** `feat/week7-evidence-provenance`
**Target under test:** the Neon dev database (read-only queries; no writes, no UI session)
**Scope requested:** take one NGO and one donor, walk the complete platform lifecycle, report how the platform is behaving today.

**Method note:** this was traced against live data rather than clicked through a browser,
because every `/admin/*` page redirects without an ADMIN session and Claude does not enter
credentials. Reading the rows each stage would have written is a stronger check than a
screenshot anyway: it shows whether a stage has *ever* run, not just whether a page renders.

The pair chosen is genuinely linked, not two unrelated examples:

- **NGO — Kiran Welfare Society** (`9283e7da`), the organisation furthest along the chain.
- **Donor — Vaibhavi** (`1edad131`), the donor who actually funded that NGO's project.

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 The compliance badges on Kiran Welfare Society are backwards

| Field | Status | Confidence | Matches form | Flag earned |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `a12Number` | VALIDATED | **0** | — | `a12Verified: true` |
| `eightyGNumber` | VALIDATED | 0.95 | — | `eightyGVerified: true` |
| `orgName` | EXTRACTED | 0.99 | true | — |
| `panNumber` | EXTRACTED | 0.99 | true | `panVerified: **false**` |
| `registrationNumber` | EXTRACTED | 0.99 | true | `registrationVerified: **false**` |

`a12Number` is VALIDATED at **confidence 0**, and there is no 12A document among the
organisation's three analyses (registration certificate, PAN card, 80G certificate). An
admin validated a field the extractor never read, and that empty validation is what lights
the "12A verified" badge. Meanwhile the two fields that *do* have strong evidence — PAN and
registration number, both 0.99 and both matching the registration form — have their flags
off.

So the badge with no evidence behind it is ON, and the two with evidence are OFF. The
evidence gate held *procedurally* (a human did click validate) but it cannot distinguish a
real validation from a reflexive one, which is the assumption the whole
`lib/compliance-evidence.ts` design rests on.

Related, on two other organisations:
- **Sparsh Sewa Samiti** (`ac16877b`) has 2 VALIDATED fields with real confidence and
  matching values, but **no `NGOCompliance` row at all** — validated evidence earning no
  badge, the mirror image of Kiran. It also has 3 extracted fields and **0 document
  analyses**, which should not be possible.
- **Anmol Vikas Trust** (`2f8a87ea`) is VERIFIED with zero documents, zero analyses and zero
  extracted fields — the "Not analysed" case, correctly rendered as such but still approved.

### 2.2 Tejamma is VERIFIED while carrying three HIGH defects

Its documents name **"Prerna Vikas Foundation"**; the organisation is registered as
"Tejamma". All five extracted fields are NEEDS_REVIEW, with `orgName`, `panNumber` and
`registrationNumber` all at `matchesSubmitted: false`. Three HIGH `VERIFICATION_DEFECT`
alerts are open and its `RiskReview` is `ESCALATED` / HIGH.

Triage did exactly its job. A human approved the organisation anyway. This is the third
recorded occurrence of that pattern, and it is worth being precise about what it means: the
verification gate is **advisory, not blocking**. Nothing in the code prevents approving an
organisation whose documents describe a different entity.

### 2.3 No real payment has ever moved through this database

All three donations carry `pay_mock_*` payment ids, and `WebhookEvent` is **empty**. The
Razorpay webhook is the designated source of payment truth, and it has never processed a
delivery here. The idempotency, captured-amount and refund paths built in Week 6 are unit
tested but have never run against a real delivery.

### 2.4 The headline numbers on project pages are fiction

- Project `raisedAmount` = **253,000** against a ledger of **3,000** → `PROJECT_TOTAL_MISMATCH`, open.
- CSR Demo Donor `totalDonated` = **210,000** with **zero** donation rows → `DONOR_TOTAL_MISMATCH`, open.

Both are seeded counters rather than a code defect — and both were caught. The reconciler is
the only thing in the platform asserting those numbers are wrong, which is precisely what it
was built for.

### 2.5 Week 7's entire subject matter has no data

Zero milestone proofs and zero milestone reviews exist platform-wide; all four milestones are
PENDING. The GPS provenance check, the Gemini proof validation, the admin review queue and
the duplicate-detection check added this week have never seen a single row.

### 2.6 The Week 7 structural gap, visible in the data

Kiran's funded allocation (₹31,000, "Mobile health camp — monsoon quarter") has **no
milestones and no project**. Its three milestones belong to the *donation*-funded
after-school project. Funded work and submitted evidence are two unconnected object graphs —
exactly the gap SPEC-3 of [`WEEK7-BLUEPRINT.md`](WEEK7-BLUEPRINT.md) exists to close.

### 2.7 Documents are not where the profile says they are

On all four NGOs, `NGOProfile.registrationCertUrl` / `panCardUrl` / `certificate12AUrl` /
`certificate80GUrl` are **NULL**, while the real files sit on
`NgoDocumentAnalysis.documentUrl` as `/api/documents/documents/<uuid>.pdf` (private storage,
session-gated — correct). Any surface still reading the profile columns will report "no
documents" for an organisation that has three.

## 3. How the platform is behaving, in one paragraph

The **marketplace half is real**: opportunity → deterministic matching → proposal → allocation
approval → confirmed payment works end to end for one organisation, with ledger entries on
both sides and a reconciler that catches counter drift. The **accountability half is a
shell**: documents verify, but the badge logic mislabels what was actually proven; donations
record, but no receipt is ever issued and no webhook has ever run; milestones exist, but no
evidence has ever been submitted against them. The platform can currently prove *who was
promised money* far better than it can prove *what was done with it* — which is the inverse
of what it sells.

## 4. Recommended order

1. **Fix the compliance-badge inversion (§2.1).** A live correctness bug on a trust signal,
   and arguably more urgent than any Week 7 spec.
2. **Apply the `contentHashes` migration** so SPEC-1 can run at all.
3. **Get one proof into the system** so the evidence leg has data.
4. **SPEC-3**, which closes §2.6 and makes the week's theme true.
